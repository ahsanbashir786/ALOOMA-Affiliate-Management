# Staff guide – the daily routine

Open **Today's Work**. Each row answers four things: **who** (name and relationship), **why now** (coloured chips and one line of evidence),
**what to do** (the buttons) and **by when** (the deadline chip on the right). Open **Details** for the full reasons, sample timeline, last actions and the message.

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Everything due, plus the most important sample cases and a few opportunities |
| **Due & overdue** | Promises and follow-ups that need you now |
| **To verify** | Possible content waiting for a yes or no |
| **Samples** | Delivered samples with nothing posted by the deadline |
| **Opportunities** | Recruit (new creators), Develop (existing creators, no sample needed), Reactivate (gone quiet) |
| **Scheduled / Paused** | Already handled – waiting for their date, or on hold |

A creator can have several promises at once. Each is shown separately on the row (for example *Bedsheet · video 1/2 · 1d late · due 2 Nov*) –
a later promise never hides an earlier one.

## Each row has two controls

* **Message** – shows the ready EN/BM text (WhatsApp link). Opening WhatsApp/TikTok or copying a message does **not** count as contact.
* **Update** – record what happened:
  * **Contacted** – after you really sent a message. Saves who and when; next follow-up in 3 days.
  * **Promised date** – the creator gave a date. Pick a date; optional *Add details* (video or LIVE, product, how many). If a promise already exists choose **Move this date** or **Add another promise**.
  * **Needs help** – pick a reason and the next date.
  * **Pause** (or **Resume** on paused rows) – short reason; optional date to come back.
  * **Verify content** – appears only when the system found a possible match.
* **Undo** appears after every action. Tick several creators → **Mark selected as contacted** → confirm the count.
* **Click the creator's name** – a side panel shows commitments (with 1/2 bars and deadlines), samples (delivery timeline), evidence and history, so you never leave the list. **Open full page** is there if you want more.
* **Add creator** (top right) starts outreach to someone new; no sample needed.

Filters: *Today, Due & overdue, To verify, Samples* are always visible; **More filters** holds Opportunities, Scheduled, Paused and sample exceptions. **Affiliates** is a searchable list with one card per creator: type (video/LIVE), WhatsApp number, every product they received a sample of with its SKU, orders (V = from videos, L = from LIVE), GMV, videos posted and the last video date, total GMV, and a one-word **Status** and **Action** (hover a word to see what it means; the full list is under the table); **Detailed analysis** (SKU breakdowns) is under Analysis.

After 3 recorded contacts without a promise or new content, the row suggests **Review or pause**. The system cannot see WhatsApp replies, so it never says they did not reply.

## What you never do

Log replies, type notes (optional), or record "content posted" – the next upload finds the videos and updates the promise by itself (1/2 → 2/2).
Weekly: upload the three files in **Data hub**, then press **Refresh recommendations**.
