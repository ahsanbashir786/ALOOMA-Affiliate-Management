# Staff guide – the daily routine

Open **Daily Actions** (formerly Today's Work). Each row answers four things: **who** (name and relationship), **why now** (coloured chips and one line of evidence),
**what to do** (the buttons) and **by when** (the deadline chip on the right). Open **Details** for the full reasons, sample timeline, last actions and the message.

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Everything due, plus the most important sample cases and a few opportunities |
| **Due & overdue** | Promises and follow-ups that need you now |
| **To verify** | Possible content waiting for a yes or no |
| **Samples** | Delivered samples with nothing posted by the deadline |
| **Opportunities** | Recruit (new creators), Develop (existing creators, no sample needed), Reactivate (gone quiet) |
| **Scheduled / Paused** | Already handled – waiting for their date, or on hold |

A creator can have several promises at once. Each is shown separately on the row (for example *Bedsheet · video 1/2 · 1d late · due 2 Nov*) –
a later promise never hides an earlier one.

## Each row has two controls

* **Message** – shows the ready EN/BM text (WhatsApp link). Opening WhatsApp/TikTok or copying a message does **not** count as contact.
* **Update** – record what happened:
  * **Contacted** – after you really sent a message. Saves who and when; next follow-up in 3 days.
  * **Promised date** – the creator gave a date. Pick a date; optional *Add details* (video or LIVE, product, how many). If a promise already exists choose **Move this date** or **Add another promise**.
  * **Needs help** – pick a reason and the next date.
  * **Pause** (or **Resume** on paused rows) – short reason; optional date to come back.
  * **Verify content** – appears only when the system found a possible match.
* **Undo** appears after every action. Tick several creators → **Mark selected as contacted** → confirm the count.
* **Click the creator's name** – a side panel shows commitments (with 1/2 bars and deadlines), samples (delivery timeline), evidence and history, so you never leave the list. **Open full page** is there if you want more.
* **Add creator** (top right) starts outreach to someone new; no sample needed.

**Daily Actions now uses the same status and action as Affiliate Data Management.** Each creator is one row, with a chip per product (*Pillow · Silent · Chase*; a tick means that action was already recorded). Click a chip, or **Message**, to open the side panel with the ready message and the buttons that record what happened. The top shows **To Do, Due & Overdue, Done Today and Waiting**; **Done Today** lists every action staff recorded today and who did it. Tabs: *To Do, Due & Overdue, To Verify, Done Today*; **More filters** holds *Chase & Coach, Growth, Scheduled, Paused* and sample exceptions. The owner-only **Progress** page now shows **Staff performance** (actions, creators reached, posted after contact) and **Results after action** (sales 28 days after first contact against the 28 days before – a direction, not proof; it fills in once 28 days have passed). The **Command centre** lists creators by status and action, and every number opens the table already filtered. **Samples** Tracker and Creators are no longer in the menu (Sample products and Sample report are under Analysis). Clicking a creator opens a **Performance** section: 28-day GMV and change, an 8-week chart, posting rate, days to post, last action and next follow-up. **Affiliate data** (Affiliate Data Management) is the full creator table: one card per creator with a line for every product they received a sample of – SKU, orders and GMV each split **V** (from videos) and **L** (from LIVE), videos posted and the last video date, then the creator's total GMV. **Each line has its own Status and Action** with a short reason (for example *Winner – RM 6,861 lately · top sellers*). Click an **Action** to open the creator panel on it: it shows what to do, a ready English/Malay message with a WhatsApp button, and buttons to record what happened (*I contacted them*, *They gave a date*, *Needs help*, with Undo). What you record goes into the same history as Today's Work, so follow-ups appear there. Every filter (search, product, status, action chips with counts, and under **More filters**: samples, video/LIVE, WhatsApp, contact) applies as soon as you choose it; **Export to Excel** downloads exactly what is filtered, one row per creator × product. **Detailed analysis** (SKU breakdowns) is under Analysis.

After 3 recorded contacts without a promise or new content, the row suggests **Review or pause**. The system cannot see WhatsApp replies, so it never says they did not reply.

## What you never do

Log replies, type notes (optional), or record "content posted" – the next upload finds the videos and updates the promise by itself (1/2 → 2/2).
Weekly: upload the three files in **Data hub**, then press **Refresh recommendations**.
