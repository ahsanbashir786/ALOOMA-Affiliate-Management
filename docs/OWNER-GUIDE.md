# Owner guide – what Progress shows

Open **Progress**. Pick 7, 28 or 90 days (Malaysia time). **Every figure is a link** to the records behind it (creator, detail, date).
Staff-confirmed numbers (someone pressed a button) are kept apart from system-detected ones (found in the TikTok files).

| Section | What it tells you | Defined as |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs your decision** | Stalled relationships, long-overdue promises, possible content to confirm, unmatched or late samples, import lines to review, outdated order files | Derived from the records; each links to the list |
| **Follow-ups** | Were due follow-ups done? | One per promise plus a creator-level one, by due date. Completed only by a recorded *Contacted* (or a promised date the creator gave). **On-time rate = on time ÷ (on time + late + still overdue).** Rescheduled, paused and "resolved by content" are shown separately and earn no credit |
| **Promises** | Secured, fulfilled, partly done, overdue | Counted by current deadline. *Fulfilled* = the required number of distinct, matching pieces with a known posting date after the promise (or confirmed by staff). Moving a date is **not** a new promise |
| **Activity and output** | Different creators contacted; new content after outreach | Each creator / piece counted once. "After outreach" is **timing only** – it does not prove the contact caused it |
| **Relationships** | Working together, Selling, Approached, New, Inactive, Stalled, Paused | Derived from contacts, promises, samples and sales (limits in Settings) – never typed in |
| **Last 8 weeks** | Contacted, follow-ups on time, promises fulfilled, new content | Weeks start Monday |
| **Sales movement** | Newly selling, reactivated, sales of contacted creators | Context only. Equal-length periods, only where both order files fully cover both periods; the effective dates are shown; otherwise it says why it is withheld. A missing upload is never zero sales |

Not measured on purpose: reply rates (replies are not logged) and message counts as a judgment of performance. Sales after a contact are not credited to staff.
